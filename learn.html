UniPadel

By the player, for the player.
UniPadel exists to fix the parts of padel apps that consistently feel clunky, unclear, or unfair — without overcomplicating things.

Big picture
Most padel apps are built around bookings.
Players experience the friction.
Why this matters

Over time, the same problems show up again and again — regardless of club or platform. These aren’t edge cases. They’re structural.

The repeat offenders
  • Booking friction — too many taps, too much uncertainty
  • Matchmaking & levels — unclear or unfair outcomes
  • Discovery — clubs and pricing are hard to compare
  • Trust & clarity — rules and changes aren’t obvious
Problem 1

Booking friction

Booking a simple game often requires more thinking than the game itself.

What players feel
  • Unclear availability
  • Hidden pricing rules
  • Too many screens
What should change
  • Predictable steps
  • True cost shown early
  • Fewer decisions per screen
Problem 2

Matchmaking & levels

Players want to know why a match is suggested — not just accept it.

Problem 3

Club discovery

Players shouldn’t have to guess what a club is like before they arrive.

Problem 4

Trust & clarity

If players don’t understand the rules, they don’t trust the platform.
