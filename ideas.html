Help us beat the “booking app frustration”
UniPadel is building the padel platform players actually want. The most valuable submissions are specific pain points (especially Playtomic) with a clear expectation of what “good” looks like.
Best submissions include
  • Where it happens (booking, payments, finding games, rankings, etc.)
  • What feels broken or annoying
  • Why it’s bad (time, money, confusion, unfairness)
  • What you expected instead
