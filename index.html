About UniPadel

UniPadel is a player-driven space for sharing ideas about how padel apps and platforms could work better for the people who use them.

Many padel players rely on popular booking platforms every week, but often feel there are missing features, clunky flows, or limited ways to give meaningful feedback. UniPadel exists to capture those ideas in one place.

This is an early prototype focused on listening to players, highlighting common frustrations, and exploring how the padel experience could be improved over time.

UniPadel is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any existing padel booking platforms.
